Notes on trajectory generation using Newton method:
*Higher times ----> lower inputs, you can help by penalizing the input more
*Long flat regions ----> polynomial tends to stepwise ----> if traj difficult it becomes unstable ---> too rough, if you want to improve decrease U cost
*Cost on speed must be low ---> in the middle it is normal since it is underactuated to have big swings of which we do not care much, so why penalizing?
*In general you cannot impose arbitrarily fast maneuvers without high costs ---> underactuation forces to follow system frequency (some seconds)
*FE and RK4 are similar in our case (rk4 little better in cost, FE is faster)
*Not much sense in going up with smoothness of polynomial, the idea is to put a smooth transient, that's all

Notes on trajectory tracking LQR:
*Since deviations are tipically small, you have to emphasize them by putting high costs.
*If you put high U cost, the deviation from the right u is penalized but siince the x differ if you appply the u that was thought for the nominal 
case it provides worse tracking.
*QQT gets chosen as a LTI infinite time horizon because, at the end of the finite time horizon we think the system as in an equilibrium state, so it becomes as if the 
system was a LTI infinite from that to infinity. Hence, the P matrix is calculated as with the Algebraic Riccati equation because of this, and since it is the same for t=T 
to t=infinite, it is that P_inf is the same also for t=T (backward iteration), so we know that for that point P is P_inf. But since that point is also the terminal point of a LTV
finite horizon, it will be that PT = QQT = P_inf because it is the first step also of infinite LTI. In fact for the calculation of P inf, linearization around last point of 
ltv finite horizon is performed for the calculation of P_inf (see[]).

[The use of dare to compute the final cost weighting matrix is driven by the fact that we chose a target 
which is a equilibria of the system. Using this choce, the linearization of the non linear system around the
equilibria pair
provide a LTI system. For which then we can use the DARE to compute the P_inf, and set it as final cost matrix used for the 
backpropagation of the costate.

By doing that, Q_final, account also for all the cost from TT-1 up to infinte]
