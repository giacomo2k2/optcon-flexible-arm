\section{Conclusion}
This project successfully demonstrated the control of a flexible under-actuated link.
\begin{itemize}
\item The **analytic Jacobian** and **RK4 discretization** provided the necessary precision for optimization.
\item The **DARE-based terminal cost** significantly improved the stability of finite-horizon approximations.
\item The **Dynamic Horizon Extension** strategy was the key enabler for generating complex trajectories, effectively bypassing local minima issues common in nonlinear optimization.
\item Finally, **LQR and MPC** proved effective for tracking, with MPC offering the critical advantage of handling actuator constraints.
\end{itemize}
